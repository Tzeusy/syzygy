**Finding 4 — Interaction with the signed `no-currency-bound-declared` scenario is unstated** (note)
`no-currency-bound-declared` is RFC2-24 reason 3 and is dismissable under the paragraph (the packet says so, OWNER-DECISION-PACKET.md:211-215). The signed scenario (spec.md:470-495) makes the outside-slot disclosure the complete presentation where freshness is named, and keeps such members out of the per-freshness counts, in a separate count so that "those counts and that count together equal its membership". The paragraph says a dismissed claim's freshness "stay visible and unchanged" and that dismissed members remain in "every per-label, tier, freshness and reason count" (spec.md.patch lines 32-47 area). For this one condition there is no freshness value and no per-freshness count to remain in, and the separate count is not named. Read literally nothing is violated (remain means not leave; the signed scenario still binds), and I found no reading that forces a freshness value. But the packet sentence "the dismissal leaves its freshness ... visible" is inaccurate for this reason, and a short clause naming the outside-slot disclosure and its member count as retained would close the VIS-2 question of a dismissed no-currency member dropping out of the membership sum. The same scenario's secondary owner-contradiction route stays visible (secondary reasons are kept); the paragraph excludes only a contradicted primary reason.

**Finding 5 — A readable-but-unparseable item, or a partly readable record set, has no stated class** (note)
Criterion 8 asks that every reachable record state falls in exactly one class. The paragraph covers a record set that "cannot be read" (all counts Unknown) and records that lack fields (refused, listed "with its record identity"). A file in the designated home that is not parseable as a record at all has no record identity and may lack every field; and "cannot read that record set" does not say whether one unreadable member makes the whole set unreadable or is skipped. Skipping would let an evaluation leave a record out by not reading it, which the paragraph elsewhere forbids ("an evaluation cannot leave a record out by not reading it", SEMANTIC-DELTA.md) and would show a zero refused count over an incomplete set. Name the rule (unparseable item counts toward the Unknown record-set condition, or is refused with an unstated identity).

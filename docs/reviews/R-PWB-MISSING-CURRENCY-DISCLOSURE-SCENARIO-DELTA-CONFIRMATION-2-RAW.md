## Findings

**Finding 1 — aggregate freshness for a member with no bound is undefined, not failed closed** (revise)
Evidence: spec.md:448 and 462 ("Aggregates SHALL disclose label, tier, freshness..."; "aggregate label/tier/freshness/reason counts"); spec.md:629 (the performed opening aggregate "discloses its own label, tier, freshness", unconditional); RFC6-17 (RFC-0006-cross-surface-selection-query-drawer.md:324-333, "per-freshness-state counts"; the full composition is the RFC6-22 tuple); proposed/spec.md.patch:+14-+17; SEMANTIC-DELTA.md Downstream impact; OWNER-DECISION-PACKET.md:87-90 ("how the aggregate's freshness reads then is not decided here").
Analysis of the open question. The text does not turn the condition green: it forbids favourable absorption and a fifth value, and RFC2-10 closes the vocabulary. It also does not fail closed to Unknown, for four reasons.
(a) The exception reads "for this condition only... where this requirement otherwise names freshness". The condition is a claim's class lacking a bound. The aggregate sentence is part of "this requirement", but the aggregate is not a claim whose class lacks a bound. It is unclear whether the aggregate's freshness slot is excused, shown as an outside-slot disclosure, or computed from the other members.
(b) A mixed aggregate is the dangerous case. RFC6-17 needs per-freshness-state counts. A member with no freshness value belongs to no bucket, so the buckets sum to less than the membership count, or the aggregate shows "fresh: n" with the unbounded members silently missing. The scenario's last clause names only "current or favourable value", the primary reason count and the route. It does not require the aggregate to disclose how many members sit in the outside-slot condition, or that bucket totals plus that disclosure reconcile to membership. The all-members-unbounded case can read as zero counts, which VIS-2 forbids as a reading of "no evidence".
(c) The implementation context shows why this matters. `unknownClaim` stamps `freshness: FRESH` on every Unknown claim (packages/three-surface-poc-core/src/project-shape-model.ts:190-194), and the aggregate type carries a freshness-bearing claim (same file:228-233). Nothing in the proposed text rules out an aggregate derived from those stamped values, apart from the loose "favourable" wording.
(d) The author concedes the composition is undecided (OWNER-DECISION-PACKET.md:87-90). But the delta commits (SEMANTIC-DELTA.md Composition) to being consistent with the opening aggregate, and criteria 6 and 11 require it. An unreconciled "SHALL disclose freshness" beside an exception whose reach is unclear is a drafting defect the sign-off would freeze.
Required repair: state the aggregate rule in the scenario. For example, an aggregate whose members include the condition discloses, outside the per-freshness counts, the count of members under that condition, with the per-freshness counts plus that count equal to membership. It never derives a freshness value from the other members and never shows the unbounded members as zero. Or say expressly that aggregate freshness is out of scope and that the PWB-REQ-007 aggregate sentence and spec.md:629 are unchanged and not excused.

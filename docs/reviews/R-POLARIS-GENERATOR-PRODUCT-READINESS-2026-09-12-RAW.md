## Product findings

[Observed] The candidate requires genuine generalized generation rather than a bundle formatter. Requirement 002 requires an argued account from admitted premises. Requirement 005 records actual understanding, narrative construction, review and repair stages. Requirement 006 makes inventory preparation part of bounded work rather than owner homework. Requirement 014 rejects synthetic-only runs and requires synthesis across verbose or scattered sources in at least one real evaluation. The delivery proposal explicitly keeps CLI mechanics subordinate to the actual owner experience.

[Observed] The schema boundary is implementable without inheriting the Butlers renderer's offset model. Requirement 019 and SCHEMA-CONTRACT distinguish requested assets from produced assets, provider-local handles from issued identity, captured artifact revision from evaluation, and structure from fidelity/authority. The explicit PWB compatibility adapter preserves existing wire semantics and reports unsupported adaptation. Exact executable field encoding and canonicalization remain implementation decisions to review, as the schema contract explicitly states; their absence is not by itself a product-specification defect.

[Observed] The diagram obligations address the user's meaning-first request. Curated diagrams carry factual-edge support, element markings, equivalent text and honest provenance. Supported relationship cycles are representable. Required graphics cannot be satisfied by decorative approximation, text substitution or optional output of the same kind. Computed references remain explicitly unavailable where the existing renderer cannot support their full contract. The delivery proposal correctly calls for a data-only graph renderer beyond current simple flows.

[Observed] Concise reading and fidelity are coupled, not traded off. Requirement 004 requires each stopping depth to answer its own questions and forbids deeper source text from rescuing a misleading summary. Requirement 006 reviews the complete admitted-source denominator in both directions, independently of author-selected claims. This catches the important counterexample in which every generated sentence has a valid citation but a material source trade-off disappears entirely.

[Observed] The owner flow is concrete enough to implement: selected project and known preset, clear source/provider/budget scope, automatic preparation of records, reuse of effective approvals, actual stage progress, review and repair, and preserved authored predecessor. It does not require users to construct scheduler records or manifests. Readiness and authorship remain separate states.

[Observed] Navigation, glossary, source paths and exported/machine presentation have explicit shared-identity and semantic obligations. Exact leaves cannot be replaced with a model paraphrase. Draft preview is permitted separately from protected publication, so an unresolved primary-file write need not prevent reading and repairing an actual generated candidate.

[Observed] Completion requires two admitted real domains, unchanged generation path, independent pre-draft source-backed questions, actual cold-reader and visual judgments, and source-change regeneration. Existing PWB walkthrough validity remains separate. A successful API call, fixture suite, authored Butlers page or internal verdict therefore cannot satisfy the stated product objective.

## Implementation checkpoints, not new specification gates

1. Give optional component deep dives explicit parent/question/return semantics in executable schema and UI tests. They can be composed from existing narrative sections and supporting assets; a seventh asset kind is unnecessary. The generation kit already identifies this handoff. Demonstrate an actually generated focused explanation and a return path, rather than counting a full-source disclosure as the requested mini deep dive.
2. Freeze executable schema, prompt pack and adapter versions before the first real portability evaluation. Record the same identities across both projects and the source-change runs. Per-project profile inputs are allowed; source-specific prompt edits or application-embedded repaired prose are not. The proposed module placements do not establish this result by themselves.
3. Make the regeneration evidence deliberately cross-asset: change a qualification or relationship used in prose, a diagram, glossary/deep dive and source link. Verify affected artifacts and review bindings together. Merely changing the snapshot digest or producing different prose is not evidence that dependent meaning was repaired.
4. Preserve full-source fidelity review under actual context/output budgets. Chunking or staged inventory preparation is a normal implementation choice, but record its complete source population and unresolved chunks. Passing a review on the model's selected excerpts cannot discharge requirement 006.
5. Evaluate the full owner flow and complete page, including representative middle/deepest sections and narrow/keyboard traversal. The current visual primitives are reusable assets, not evidence that generated output is polished.

These checkpoints operationalize existing obligations. They do not call for a new scheduler, generic authorization platform, extra asset quota or an additional approval ceremony.

## Readiness conclusion and limits

[Inferred] The inspected product contract is sufficiently concrete to guide a reviewable implementation once its separate authority and applicable-contract gates are resolved. Further blocking claims should identify a specific contradictory or underdetermined observable behavior, rather than equating missing implementation with missing specification.

[Unknown] Actual provider selection/admission, a second project's source admission, executable schema/runner/renderer behavior, generated output quality and portability remain unproven. This review grants none of them. The generalized-generator goal remains incomplete.

**Finding 2 — the registry digest-path argument is sound; its label is conservative and one corroborating sentence is uncited** (note)

Checked against the code and RFC. `body-read-authority.ts` rejects a record kind other than an owner-act record (`git-ref-only`: "only a Git commit/tag attests"; `specification-signoff-only`) and, for an owner-act record, requires `RFC3-16(b) item 3` exact digest (`exact-digest-missing`, `exact-digest-malformed`, `exact-digest-wrong`) and a `PHRASE: <sha256>` ceremony phrase (`phrase-missing`, `phrase-malformed`). RFC3-16(a) at `RFC-0003/governance-homes-and-owner-acts.md` reads: "Such an artifact is honored only through an **effective owner act**." and "An effective owner act is an actual human owner act whose record is current, attributable, scope-matched and bound to the artifact's exact digest under RFC3-16(b)." The packet's quotation is accurate. A tag-signed sign-off (see `PWB-READABILITY-SUCCESSOR-SIGNOFF-v1.0.md`, "Tag: pwb-readability-successor-v1.0") carries neither digest nor phrase, so the gate would hold the registry role invalid. [Observed] for the gate behaviour; the packet's [Inferred] label on the conclusion "no lawful re-point would exist" is correct for the conclusion and merely conservative for its premises. The reasoning is accurate to RFC3-16 and to the gate.

The Scope A direction (`OWNER-DIRECTION-VERSIONED-SIGNOFF-SCOPE-A-2026-10-02.md`, "What this does not do") also says: "Until that change lands, the existing phrase-and-digest acts remain the only way to perform the queued packages." That sentence independently supports keeping the registry on a digest act and is not cited in the packet's "Signing path". Citing it would make the argument not depend only on an inference. The policy being outside Scope A is correct: item 1 enumerates "the PWB specification deltas, the observer registry entry and the contract successors queued behind them", and a secret-classification policy is none of them.

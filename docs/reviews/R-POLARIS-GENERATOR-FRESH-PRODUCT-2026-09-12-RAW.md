achieved by simplifying presentation, never content." Requirement 004's

[Observed] RFC7-21 says, "The adopter attests per claim block" and "Adoption

computed production instead of relabeling an editorial drawing as computed.

Requirement 013 and OWNER-FLOW.md retain this seam and distinguish subordinate
generation controls from scheduler-owned lifecycle.

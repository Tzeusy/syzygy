## Findings

**Finding 1 — The narrowing and expansion arms cannot tell a working filter from a no-op** (note) [Inferred]
Under this specification every served item's `governedBy` is the same Unknown, so the oracle's narrowing arm ("The narrowed board lists exactly the items the un-narrowed board renders, since every one carries the Unknown") is passed by a "narrow" control that does nothing, and the fixture cannot include a non-Unknown item to discriminate. This is the same reachability class the delta used to remove the edge arm and round 2 used against the orphaned-work SHALL, but milder: a broken filter that drops or adds items does fail, and with an all-Unknown population a no-op is behaviourally identical to a correct filter in every reachable state. The RFC8-23 covered row ("filterable within Trajectory's rendered selection") and the RFC8-24 aggregation row ("expand to their members within Trajectory's rendered selection … the narrowed board is the expansion") are honest about what is checked but do not say that the filter's discriminating power is unexercised. Suggested: one clause in each row (or in the delta's §"How each Trajectory arm is decided") saying the filter is exercised only over an all-Unknown population, so filtering *between* states stays unobserved until an edge amendment admits a warranted item. Not a contradiction with criterion 3.

**Finding 2 — The resolution-statement procedure has a judgment step, and the delta leans on RFC8-31 without anchoring it** (note) [Observed: text; Inferred: consequence]
The oracle's literal parts are mechanical (reason name, its RFC2-24 route, the word "warrant"), but "in a statement that supplying it resolves an item" is a reading, not a literal: a region saying "no warrant" next to the reason and route passes the literal test. The delta concedes "The exact wording is the implementation's; the literal parts are not", which is a fair disclosure; a checker still needs a stated rule (for example a declared sentence or a declared key phrase) for the last clause to be a CC-SPEC-4 "stated procedure" end to end. Separately, the delta's procedure cites "read as text alone (RFC8-31)" without quoting the clause (verification rule 8) and the spec's oracle says "Read as text alone" without a warrant to RFC8-31; RFC8-31 stays mapped only to POC-REQ-060/061. Neither changes a covered row; both are wording residuals.

**Finding 3 — Two "outside" figures may appear on Trajectory** (note) [Inferred]
Signed POC-REQ-042's oracle reconciles "rendered count + excluded count equals the total count read directly from Dolt at the served revision"; POC-REQ-014's reconciles inside + outside against the served Unknown count, which by POC-REQ-011 is the prefix-scoped served set. Where the Dolt total and the served set coincide (as the current code's `excludedCount = workItems.items.length - rendered.length` suggests, observed by round 2), one figure serves both. Where they differ, the two requirements demand two different "outside" numbers beside each other. That is not a contradiction — both can be shown — but neither the requirement nor the delta says whether POC-REQ-014's outside figure is POC-REQ-042's excluded count or a second figure, and a reader of Trajectory could see two disagreeing "outside" counts (VIS-3). A Scope sentence would close it.

## Findings

**Finding 1 — Applying the package makes the repository's contract-coverage check fail; a sixth subject (`CONTRACT-COVERAGE.md`) is missing from the proposed set** (blocking)

`proposed/CONTRACT-COVERAGE-REPAIR-DELTA.md.patch` lines for RFC7-14.r2, RFC7-26.r1 and RFC7-26.r2 write `covered:PWB-REQ-013;PWB-REQ-015`. The repository's grammar is comma-only: `scripts/build_polaris_project_wide_contract_coverage.py:33` `COVERED_DISPOSITION = r"covered:PWB-REQ-\d{3}(?:,PWB-REQ-\d{3})*"`, and its own selftest (lines ~510-520) asserts the semicolon form is an "unparseable repair row". Reproduced in a scratch copy with all five patches applied: `build_polaris_project_wide_contract_coverage.py --check` raises `ValueError: unparseable repair row at line 84 ... covered:PWB-REQ-013;PWB-REQ-015`. The same check passes on the unmodified c1612d3 tree, so the package causes the failure. Repairing the separator to a comma (scratch only) does not clear it: the check then reports `DRIFT: CONTRACT-COVERAGE.md differs from verified matrices`, because the derived summary changes when the dispositions change. The manifest keeps `CONTRACT-COVERAGE.md` as an unchanged row, and the ledger and `SEMANTIC-DELTA.md` ("Five signed subjects move") and the packet ("No row changes status") do not disclose a sixth moving subject. The builder's `--check` and `--selftest` do not run this neighbouring generator, so they report green. Needed: comma separators, regenerate `CONTRACT-COVERAGE.md` (and any derived matrix), add it as a proposed row, restate the "five/six unchanged" counts, and add the generator's `--check` to the package's own predicate set.

**Finding 2 — The relation claim has no admitted source: PWB-REQ-004's closed fact population and the nine extraction classes contain no governing-relation declaration** (revise)

Amended PWB-REQ-015 requires "captured declared governing relations" and a relation claim, but PWB-REQ-004 (spec.md:825-) says the POC admits project facts "only from this closed population" (`item:`/`count:` for the nine extraction classes, `catalog-count:`, `project-account:`) and that "an injected or unrecognized fact, class, key, catalog or account key SHALL mint nothing". PWB-REQ-002 closes the extraction classes at nine, and none reads an item-to-requirement/non-goal/doctrine mapping. Under the applied text either (a) the relation claim is an unrecognized fact and mints nothing, or (b) no relation can ever be "captured", so every item is `missing-declaration` and the Observed arm, the plural arm and the sweep cases ("uniquely mapped", "two compatible mappings") are unsatisfiable. The ledger lists PWB-REQ-007, 011, 013, 014, 016, 020 as the reached authority but never PWB-REQ-004 or PWB-REQ-002, and no open point discloses this. The semantic delta must say where a governing relation is declared, which extractor emits it and under which class/key (or amend the closed population explicitly), or name this as an open point.

**Finding 3 — The plural-relation arm is not total: "excludes" is undefined and a mixed population has no result** (revise)

Criterion 3 asks that every population of captured relations get exactly one deterministic result. Drafted text: Observed when the relations are "none of which excludes another"; Unknown `contradicted-pending-adjudication` for "mutually exclusive captured governing relations". Counterexamples. (i) Three relations where A excludes B and C is compatible with both: the first arm fails (A excludes B); the second arm ("mutually exclusive") is satisfiable only pairwise-for-all or ambiguously, so the population has no stated result. (ii) Neither the text nor the oracle says what "excludes" means, who declares an exclusion, or how it is determined without inferring from prose, which the same paragraph forbids ("SHALL NOT infer a relation from a label, basename, similarity or generated prose"). Two independent channels could therefore decide a requirement-plus-non-goal pair differently (the sweep asserts it is compatible but gives no rule). (iii) PWB-REQ-004 lets a Butlers-declared precedence outcome select an effective statement; the draft makes any exclusion `contradicted-pending-adjudication` without saying whether a precedence outcome resolves it. Open point 1 discloses only the Observed-set versus all-contradiction choice, not this totality gap. Needed: a closed, declaration-based definition of exclusion and an explicit rule for mixed populations, or fall back to the owner's other arm.

**Finding 4 — Capability-to-catalog-item matching is undefined; a capability with no (or several) matching items loses its deep dive** (revise)

Amended PWB-REQ-015 makes the capability deep dive "that reading for the matching declared catalog item; no second detail or identity SHALL be created", and confines proposal material to "an item detail for a matching declared capability". Neither the spec text nor the design says how a declared capability matches a catalog item (the delta says capability identity is "declared, never inferred from a catalog row", which excludes label matching). If a declared capability matches zero catalog items, today's conformant deep dive has no home and PWB-REQ-013 ("Proposed work SHALL appear only in the affected capability's detail") would be unsatisfiable for its proposal; if it matches two items, "no second detail" cannot hold. Not disclosed in the open points or the choices table (the "Existing capability" row addresses only the one-to-one case). Needed: state the matching declaration and the zero/many behaviour, or disclose as an open point.

**Finding 5 — "Renders verbatim" versus "verbatim-reachable", and open point 2 names only gate failures** (revise)

The contract-band sentence says it "SHALL contain only captured governing identities and verbatim-reachable current requirement/scenario, ... text", but the next sentences and the Scenario say the band "renders each related current intent verbatim". PWB-REQ-011 permits body encoding only through the exact-source route's gated, transient path in two closed modes (`requirement-sections` for baseline specs, `whole-body` otherwise), with no unselected body bytes, and its units are sections or whole bodies, not single requirement or non-goal items. Inline verbatim rendering of every related intent in every item detail is a second body-encoding site with unspecified granularity, and doctrine/non-goal sources would be `whole-body`. Further, open point 2 describes only a failed authority/exact-object/secret/inert gate; the same question arises, with a different cause, when the related source is excluded (digest-only), missing or unreadable (PWB-REQ-003, PWB-REQ-011 "Withheld source stays digest-only"). The disclosure is accurate but incomplete. Needed: make "reachable through the exact-source route" the only obligation or define inline rendering within PWB-REQ-011's modes, and widen open point 2 to every non-served outcome.

**Finding 6 — PWB-REQ-010 and PWB-REQ-011 still speak only of capability depth** (note)

PWB-REQ-011 lists levels "project summary, complete project catalogs, capability deep dives and exact authoritative artifacts"; PWB-REQ-010 orders the opening "before presenting any single capability's detail". Under the amendment a non-capability item detail is a reading level neither names, so PWB-REQ-010's ordering guard does not literally cover it and PWB-REQ-011's level list is narrower than the path its own sweep ("follow one declared item through each available depth") now has. Neither becomes false (the ledger's "available depth" argument holds), but the unchanged wording will read stale to a fresh reader; consider a one-line disclosure or a later wording amendment.

Counting `^[+-]` lines over every `.patch` of the six applied packages, `pwb-dismissal-expiry-amendment/proposed/GOVERNING-DEPENDENCIES.md.patch` changes two lines naming PWB-REQ-015 (the VIS-4 row, which only gains PWB-REQ-007). The other 11 files give 0. The substance (no requirement-text overlap) holds; the stated figure and population should be restated as "no spec.md hunk" or note the generated dependency row.

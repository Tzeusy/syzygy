# Proposed amendment: discovery and project understanding

Candidate behavioral delta for `polaris-generation`; binds nothing. This is an
additive amendment design, not replacement text for the adopted specification.
All existing requirements, scenarios, safety boundaries and completion obligations
remain. No implementation of newly introduced behavior is authorized by this file.

[Observed] Baseline: commit `cb0614a`, with the adopted specification at
`openspec/changes/polaris-manifesto-generation/specs/polaris-generation/spec.md`.
The executable intermediate records and scripted demo do not satisfy the full
specification. In particular, independent source understanding and real-project
quality evidence are already required by REQ-polaris-generation-006/014.

## D1 — Accounted discovery

Proposed clause for REQ-polaris-generation-001:

The generator SHALL produce an inspectable discovery scope before claiming source
coverage for an unfamiliar project. That scope SHALL identify the selected project
boundary, admitted source classes, supported interpretation capabilities, inspected
material, selected material and the reasons relevant material is excluded,
unavailable, unresolved or deferred by budget. Discovery SHALL NOT assume a
prescribed document layout or treat a retrieved subset as the complete repository.
A source scope change SHALL create a new identified input and retire dependent
coverage conclusions. Every additional read remains subject to the existing
per-repository and content-class authority checks.

### Scenario D1.1 — Purpose outside the obvious entry point

- **WHEN** an independently prepared case places material purpose and architecture
  information in permitted sources outside its README and conventional directories
- **THEN** the discovery account identifies the relevant material and its role in
  the understanding, or explicitly reports the unsupported discovery boundary
- **AND** an apparently complete account based only on the entry point fails the
  evaluation when that omitted material changes its meaning.

### Scenario D1.2 — Partial access and finite research

- **WHEN** a material question needs a denied source, unsupported interpreter or
  more research than the remaining budget permits
- **THEN** the run records that question, the applicable reason and affected scope
- **AND** it neither reads the denied content nor substitutes lack of retrieval for
  absence of a capability, source or qualification
- **AND** the owner may inspect the supported partial account without it claiming
  whole-project coverage
- **AND** a later accepted narrower scope is a separately identified request; it
  does not convert failure to satisfy the original requested scope into success.

### Scenario D1.3 — Multiple applications in one repository

- **WHEN** discovery identifies several potential project boundaries and the
  selected profile does not determine which is the requested subject
- **THEN** the owner sees the candidate boundaries and why selection matters
- **AND** no single-project thesis or relationship claim is silently constructed
  by merging unrelated packages; declared child relationships retain existing rules.

## D2 — Reviewable project understanding

Proposed clauses for REQ-polaris-generation-002 and record refinement under 019:

Before drafting the central argument, the generator SHALL expose a reviewable
understanding containing supported purpose/beneficiary candidates, concepts,
capabilities, consequential relationships, material qualifications, contradictory
accounts and unanswered questions. Each substantive item SHALL retain its source
premises, scope and existing epistemic classification. Declared intent, observed
implementation and execution state SHALL remain distinguishable. A relationship
in code SHALL NOT establish an owner's motive or an intended product promise.
Resolution of disagreement SHALL identify its applicable warrant; no implicit
newest-source or most-polished-source rule is introduced.

### Scenario D2.1 — Promise and implementation disagree

- **WHEN** admitted intent declares offline operation and separately admitted
  implementation evidence establishes a required remote dependency
- **THEN** the account distinguishes the intended promise from observed behavior
  and retains the disagreement with its sources
- **AND** the manifesto cannot pass by dropping the qualification, declaring the
  intention invalid without authority, or presenting the promise as shipped.

### Scenario D2.2 — Plausible meaning is not established meaning

- **WHEN** component names and code behavior suggest a beneficiary or business
  motive that no admitted premise supports
- **THEN** that motive remains unestablished and can prompt clarification
- **AND** plausible prose and an Inferred label do not make it eligible as fact.

### Scenario D2.3 — Reader challenges a synthesized relationship

- **WHEN** a reader inspects why the draft connects a capability to the thesis
- **THEN** the supporting premises and inference provenance are reachable
- **AND** a factual edge with individually supported endpoints but no supported
  relationship is identified as a finding, not accepted through citation presence.

## D3 — Consequential owner clarification

Proposed clauses extending REQ-polaris-generation-020 and input records under 019:

Where investigation cannot establish an answer material to the requested account,
the generator SHALL present a bounded, prioritized clarification identifying the
unknown question, evidence considered, and consequence for the draft. It SHALL
accept an attributed answer, a correction, deferral or an explicit choice to leave
it unknown. An answer SHALL be classified as owner input, presentation preference
or another applicable existing class rather than silently becoming source evidence
or adopted intent. Repeated requests for the same unchanged question SHALL reuse
its recorded disposition. The owner SHALL NOT have to prepare an inventory or
outline to start ordinary generation.

The proposed default is to resolve an unsupported central thesis before presenting
it confidently. The workflow MAY expose a clearly limited architecture/account
preview while clarification is pending. This does not make a partial preview a
completed manifesto. This interview timing is a proposed owner-experience choice,
not a permission grant or a settled adoption decision.

### Scenario D3.1 — Mechanics without purpose

- **WHEN** the admitted repository supports an architectural explanation but
  supplies no sufficient purpose or beneficiary premises
- **THEN** Polaris asks the consequential purpose question with its scope and
  reason, while preserving useful supported findings
- **AND** it offers no fabricated mission or founder story as a default fact.

### Scenario D3.2 — Owner declines or provides new intent

- **WHEN** the owner leaves the purpose unknown
- **THEN** the resulting account retains that limitation and no confidence badge
  or completion claim covers it
- **WHEN** the owner instead supplies a purpose statement
- **THEN** its attribution, revision and permitted draft use are recorded
- **AND** the statement neither proves implementation behavior nor adopts itself
  into project intent without the existing applicable act and write checks.

### Scenario D3.3 — Clarification changes meaning

- **WHEN** an attributed answer changes or is withdrawn
- **THEN** the affected thesis, explanations, diagrams, terminology, deep dives
  and reviews are identified through their dependencies and reevaluated
- **AND** prior generated content cannot silently regain eligibility by retaining
  the same question identifier or an obsolete owner answer.

### Scenario D3.4 — Questions cannot become an endless interview

- **WHEN** optional uncertainties remain after the declared question or research
  budget is exhausted, or an unchanged question was already deferred
- **THEN** the owner sees the remaining scope and can continue with a limited
  account or stop; the process does not repeat questions or extend spending
- **AND** consequential unresolved facts remain explicit in the affected reading.

## D4 — An argument and purposeful supporting artifacts

Proposed clauses extending REQ-polaris-generation-004, with asset decisions under 003:

The editorial plan SHALL identify its central reader question, supported thesis,
progression of explanations, material tensions, stopping-depth obligations and
reasons for diagram/deep-dive selection. It SHALL identify how major capabilities
contribute to the account rather than merely enumerate features. Each factual
visual relationship SHALL carry support; each deep dive SHALL answer a distinct
question and add useful explanation beyond its parent. The manifesto's composition
SHALL follow the project and audience within the adopted primary altitude order and required
per-depth contents. Unless the owner rules otherwise, thesis/manifesto,
architecture story, capability catalog, capability deep dive and verbatim leaf
remain in that order. Capability deep dives retain argument/contract/reality bands
and their distinct authority classes and populations. Flexible editorial headings
or optional artifacts SHALL NOT remove those obligations.

### Scenario D4.1 — Accurate catalog, unsuccessful explanation

- **WHEN** a draft lists correct features but independent readers cannot explain
  why the project exists or how those features support its purpose
- **THEN** argument/comprehension remains failed despite accurate individual facts
- **AND** repair revisits the argument and necessary understanding, not merely
  headings or additional citations.

### Scenario D4.2 — Meaningful visual instead of decorative quota

- **WHEN** a long architecture explanation obscures a material relationship that
  a supported diagram can clarify
- **THEN** the author/design review identifies the question, requires a useful
  relationship treatment and checks the rendered result
- **AND** inserting boxes with supported nouns but unsupported or meaningless
  arrows cannot close the finding; a figure count is not the acceptance oracle.

### Scenario D4.3 — Concision and optional depth preserve meaning

- **WHEN** a shorter introduction would omit a qualification that reverses its
  apparent promise, or a deep dive only repeats its parent
- **THEN** the qualification remains at the affected stopping depth and the
  redundant depth is repaired or explicitly omitted where optional
- **AND** source/catalog navigation cannot become the only way to understand the
  thesis, while required exact-source access remains available.

## D5 — Research repair and dependency invalidation

Proposed clauses extending REQ-polaris-generation-006 and 009:

A finding against source selection, inventory completeness, interpretation or
argument SHALL reopen the deficient artifact and invalidate dependent conclusions
and reviews. The repair protocol SHALL distinguish research, clarification,
argument, prose, asset and rendering findings by the subject requiring change.
Further acquisition SHALL use a new admitted source identity where required and
consume the original remaining bounds or a separately authorized new run. Editing
prose against an unchanged defective inventory SHALL NOT resolve an inventory
finding. Resumption SHALL preserve prior uncertainty and existing execution rules.

### Scenario D5.1 — Omitted material qualification

- **WHEN** independent source review finds a material admitted qualification that
  both inventory and draft omitted
- **THEN** the inventory is repaired and re-reviewed against the source population;
  dependent argument, draft and asset reviews become stale
- **AND** the finding remains open if only its wording is inserted into a paragraph
  while the source-understanding defect and affected artifacts remain unchanged.

### Scenario D5.2 — New evidence outside the current budget or authority

- **WHEN** a finding requires acquisition the existing permission or remaining
  budget cannot support
- **THEN** the run records the unresolved dependency and affected readiness
- **AND** no repair loop expands access, erases spent usage or disguises a new run
  as a resumed old one.

## D6 — Evidence of generalization, including correct uncertainty

Proposed clauses extending REQ-polaris-generation-014:

The product evaluation SHALL disclose its supported repository profiles and the
limits of its generalization claim. In addition to the existing two-real-project
and changed-source obligations, evaluation SHALL include an unfamiliar held-out
project configuration and independently prepared challenge cases for sparse
intent, misleading source organization, contradictory accounts and unsupported
relationships. Expected outcomes SHALL distinguish a complete supported account,
a correct limited account, needed owner clarification and refusal. Correct
abstention SHALL NOT count as a completed manifesto. When clarification introduces new owner premises during product evaluation,
independent evaluators SHALL freeze an identified extension to the expected-answer
record from that admitted, attributed input before evaluating the revised draft.
They SHALL NOT derive the extension from generated prose or its reviewer verdict.
Material changes SHALL invalidate affected earlier evaluations; the initial case,
answer, extension and retired evaluations remain traceable. A model's own review
output SHALL NOT be the sole judge of completeness, entailment or reader comprehension.

### Scenario D6.1 — Unfamiliarity without project-specific patching

- **WHEN** the frozen engine, supported adapters, prompts and renderer are used on
  an admitted project held out from recipe tuning
- **THEN** the evaluation records input/profile differences and actual reader
  outcomes without manually fixing generated content or adding project branches
- **AND** failure remains a failure even if a bespoke page for that project can be
  produced outside the unchanged process.

### Scenario D6.2 — Correct partial account and false universal claim

- **WHEN** a case lacks purpose evidence or exceeds declared interpretation support
- **THEN** the evaluator checks the correct uncertainty, question or scope refusal
  and reports the incomplete manifesto separately
- **AND** neither success on two selected projects nor correctness on that partial
  case establishes support for every codebase.

### Scenario D6.3 — Shared model blind spot

- **WHEN** authoring and internal review agree on an account that omits an
  independently identified material source fact
- **THEN** the external evaluation fails it using the frozen source-derived oracle
- **AND** additional agreeing model verdicts do not override the missing support.

### Scenario D6.4 — Clarification changes the evaluation premises

- **WHEN** an owner answer establishes a purpose absent from the initial frozen
  evaluation sources
- **THEN** independent evaluators record its admitted attribution and freeze a
  source-derived oracle extension before judging the revised draft
- **AND** affected prior judgments become inapplicable, while the original
  insufficient-intent result remains recorded; neither producer prose nor an
  internal model verdict supplies the new expected answer.

## Impact and acceptance work still needed

[Inferred] D1 reaches acquisition/profile and source-class policy; D3 reaches
attributed input, retention, authoring and clarification controls; D5 changes
research dependency and repair behavior. The existing authorization must not be
read as adopting these proposed additions merely because they serve the same goal.

Before an amendment offer: reconcile the exact affected clauses and source classes,
resolve owner interview timing, encode changes in the existing capability's formal
amendment package while preserving bound bytes, and independently review the
current delta, failure cases and interface impacts. No new source permission,
precedence rule, global truth state, workspace/portfolio scope or autonomous intent
adoption is proposed here. Any discovery that requires one must return for an
explicitly different decision rather than be smuggled into this amendment.

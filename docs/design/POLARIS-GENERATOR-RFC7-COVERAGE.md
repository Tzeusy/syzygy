# Polaris generator: RFC7 consequence coverage

[Observed] This analysis binds the source hashes in the companion [JSON](../evidence/polaris-generator-rfc7-coverage-2026-09-12.json). It is candidate review evidence, not an adopted specification, owner non-applicability decision or implementation proof.

The source population was independently enumerated from the two installed RFC7 modules: 40 included defined headings (39 primary clauses and RFC7-11(a)); RFC7-38 is deliberately excluded. Inline RFC7-2(a-c) and RFC7-9(a-c) limbs remain inside their parent records. The current candidate contains 29 requirements and 125 named scenarios.

The 162 consequence rows reuse the older inventory only as a locator. Every source quote was checked against the current owning clause after whitespace normalization. Each selected scenario retains its exact current text, path, line and owning requirement; requirement prose and relevant approved PWB predecessor text are separately retained. Source hashes must be rechecked after edits.

**This is not a complete AR2 disposition.** The consequence units contain compound outcomes and are not asserted to exhaust every normative limb. A scenario link means the stated behavior has a concrete candidate exercise; it is not a claim that the complete parent clause is covered. Approved PWB references are bounded predecessor evidence, not automatic inheritance into generalized generation. No N/A proposal has been adopted.

[Inferred] The most useful next candidate repairs are:

- Exercise broken versus resolving-but-changed anchors separately, including exact reason/navigation/drift fields, same-evaluation parity, evaluation-only negative control and fresh-authoring-only clearance.
- Exercise every curated authoring route, including direct prose/structure edits and agent repairs; reject self-declared immateriality and preserve open wording-only classification.
- Make the generic narrative composition observable: one-step owned sources, default altitudes, the three authority bands, declared-only capability membership, and exact normative leaves without stored reordered copies.
- Extend lifecycle/rendering tests to all model-unit exports and the queryable citation graph; entry-point metadata alone does not cover every model unit.
- Exercise valid owner verdict provenance and record homes alongside existing invalid-verdict scenarios.
- Specify declared one-sided/unconfirmed project relationships; decide portfolio/workspace non-applicability explicitly rather than silently omitting it.

The newer REQ-023/024 scenarios now give direct candidate mappings for much of fixed-entry discoverability and formal walkthrough/freeze behavior that the older inventory reported missing. The rows retain narrower gaps such as exact unknown-reason branch controls and valid-judgment cases.

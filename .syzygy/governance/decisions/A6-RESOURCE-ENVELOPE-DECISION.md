**descriptive fact about existing capacity, not a promise or a spend
authorization** (the P-35 rule carries over: a stated constraint is a
fact; inventing a value is worse than recording Unknown).

## What remains open, deliberately

- **The scope-versus-capacity assessment is not performed here.** A6's
  recorded settlement is the envelope *"followed by a scope-versus-capacity
  assessment"* — that assessment is separate follow-on work, and its
  outcome is not presumed. Nothing in this record claims the V0 scope
  fits 2 hours/week; that is exactly the question the assessment must
  answer honestly.
- **No registration, onboarding, or access occurs.** Registering either
  proving project awaits its own separately authorized workflow after
  the owner's launch decision.
- **H1 is not added to the instrument.** Whether the proposed H1
  question enters a future instrument version is a separate owner
  choice.

## Boundary

This record states facts only. It authorizes no spending (the $200/month
figure is a ceiling that exists, not a budget granted), no onboarding,
no project access, no scope change, no delivery commitment, no act, no
administration, no OpenSpec authoring, and no implementation. Where this
record and the owning packet disagree, the packet's boundary wins.

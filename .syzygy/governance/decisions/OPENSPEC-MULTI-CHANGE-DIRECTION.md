# Owner direction — multiple OpenSpec changes permitted

Date: 2026-08-30

Owner: Tzeusy

Recorded from direction the owner gave directly in session on
2026-08-30. The owner's words: **"remove the requirement of 'the
prohibition on creating any OpenSpec changeset other than the adopted
Capability 1 change', as long as changes do not overlap and are
relatively well categorized I'm ok with multiple changes"**.

## What this direction changes

The standing hard prohibition on creating any OpenSpec changeset other
than the adopted Capability 1 change (and the one authorized POC
change) is **removed**. New OpenSpec changes may be created, subject to
the owner's two stated conditions:

1. **No overlap** — changes must not overlap one another in the
   behavior they specify; one behavior belongs to one change.
2. **Well categorized** — each change must be a coherent, clearly
   scoped category of behavior, not a grab-bag.

## What this direction does not change

- **Authoring is not adoption.** Every new change is a candidate and
  binds nothing until the owner signs it off (VIS-4). Creating a
  change never authorizes implementing it.
- The adopted Capability 1 change's seven artifacts and the signed
  three-surface-poc-experience artifacts remain frozen at their act
  digests; changes to either route through CC-REV-2's amendment path.
- Implementation scope remains governed by its own authorizations
  (the Capability 1 implementation-authorization act; the 2026-08-29
  POC direction and the 2026-08-30 spec sign-off act). A new spec
  change does not by itself widen implementation scope.
- The in-force craft policies (CC-SPEC-1…11, CC-IMPACT-1…7) govern
  every new change's authoring, including the CC-SPEC-8
  contract-coverage obligation.

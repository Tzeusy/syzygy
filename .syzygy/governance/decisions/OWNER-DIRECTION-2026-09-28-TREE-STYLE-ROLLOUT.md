# Owner direction — tree-style rollout across Syzygy (2026-09-28)

The owner has directed that every governed and default-path document be
restyled to CC-REV-8. Authority (doctrine, contracts, policy, adopted specs)
is restyled under this direction but adopted only by the owner's own word
(VIS-4); presentation and route pages land under the direction itself.

- **The owner's words (2026-09-28):** "Proceed with the rewrites as
  necessary; I want the whole of syzygy in line with your rewritten
  documentation".
- **Earlier words it builds on (2026-09-27):**
  - "Packets but without the extreme rigor of semantic equivalence. I trust
    your prose-translation; you have full discretion to rewrite and generate
    equivalent documents."
  - The owner approved CC-REV-8 the same day ("Approved").

## What this direction covers

- **Presentation and route pages** (READMEs, guides, plans, candidate views):
  restyles that preserve meaning land once a fresh-reader review returns
  CONFIRM, or CONFIRM WITH EXCEPTIONS with notes only (the owner's notes-only
  stopping rule of 2026-09-26).
- **Authority** (doctrine, craft and specification policy, design contracts,
  adopted specifications): the restyle is drafted, reviewed the same way, and
  offered as a packet. It binds only when the owner adopts it in their own
  words, as with D5 and D6.
- **Unperformed acts:** where a candidate's bytes are an unperformed act's
  argument (acts 2, 3 and 4), the restyle re-quotes that argument in the same
  change, as act 4's was for OVERVIEW on 2026-09-27; the owner performs the
  act later against the new bytes.

## What it does not cover

- **Changes of meaning:** a rule, scope, qualifier or permission that
  changes goes to the owner as its own packet.
- **Errors found while restyling:** a wrong citation or a stale count is
  listed in the change's packet or commit. It is not fixed silently.
- **Historical records:** raw reviews, evidence, round directories, performed
  act records and history stay byte-identical (CC-REV-6).
- **Code behavior:** none is changed by this direction.

   - doctrine answers why;

   - code, tests, CI, and runtime answer what currently exists;
   - Beads answers work lifecycle;

   - `openspec/**`;

   never separate truth stores.

   project truth surface.
9. Inference may challenge a positive claim but may not establish one.
10. The Project Genome is broader than behavioral specifications.
11. Full regeneration is a north star, not a current capability claim.
12. A real 3D map remains required in V0, with exact non-3D access.
13. Context is compiled, not accumulated.
14. Humans govern intent, constraints, risk, and budgets; agents work within
    explicitly bounded authority.
15. Human interpretability applies to doctrine, contracts, specifications,
    project views, machine interfaces, and eventual code.
16. Public clones and founder machines must tell the same current-state story.

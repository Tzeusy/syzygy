# Round-2026-08d disposition register

> **Process bookkeeping — never authority.** This register assigns a proposed
> disposition to every finding of the round's fifteen delivered reviews. It
> decides nothing, adopts nothing, and performs no act (VIS-4). A disposition
> of `repair` is a **drafting posture** in this repository's established
> sense: the repair may be drafted into candidate bytes and is ratified or
> reverted by the owner at the wave act that binds it. A disposition of
> `owner-decision` means drafting must wait for a ruling, queued in
> `decisions/PENDING-OWNER-DECISIONS.md` under the P-number given.
> **`knowingly-bind` is a disposition only the owner can select, at an act;
> it is never pre-recorded here.**
>
> Finding text authority is the raw review, `RD-<n>-…-RAW.md` beside this
> file. The tag column here is a navigation aid, never a restatement of
> record; where a tag and the raw review disagree, the raw review wins.
> The finding inventory beneath these dispositions was compiled by four
> extraction agents reading the raw files in full (2026-08-09); their
> per-review counts were reconciled against each review's own accounting,
> and the discrepancies they found are recorded in §6.
>
> **As of this register's date, no frozen subject has been edited.** Every
> review remains bound to the baseline commit `771965c` (verification
> rule 10). Repairs batch per subject area below; each wave batch ends with
> a scripted manifest regeneration and a fresh-context review of the new
> bytes, which supersedes this round's review of that wave.

## 1. Disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `repair(R-x)` | Draftable now, into batch R-x below; ratified/reverted at the owning act |

## 7. What supersession means here

When a wave batch lands, the round's review of that wave is retired for the
new bytes — its verdict remains the verdict for `771965c`, and the fresh
review of the regenerated manifest is the one an act may rely on. Nothing in
this register shortens that path: **no wave act may be offered or performed
until its wave's repairs have landed and a fresh confirming review is bound
to the new argument, or the owner has knowingly bound the finding at the
act.**

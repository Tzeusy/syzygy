## Merge and effect boundary

No candidate amended behavior, policy or registry byte may merge as current
authority before its exact owner act. The behavior package is indivisible. The
policy and registry acts are separate. Even after all three acts, implementation
resumes only after the owner explicitly continues the prior implementation
authorization across its signed-spec/policy/registry escalation triggers.

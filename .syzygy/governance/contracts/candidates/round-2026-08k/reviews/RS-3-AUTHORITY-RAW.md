The V1 measure set …" — its subject is cost measures. RFC8-19: "**Absent means Unknown, never zero** (SDR-6; RFC2-23). Every aggregate over partially-known measures discloses coverage ('cost known for n of m runs')…" — cost aggregates. Capability 1's own non-goals exclude "Trajectory work ingestion or execution accounting"; no fixture in REQ-031/034 contains a cost measure, so neither requirement's oracle can observe either clause's consequence. Under CC-SPEC-8's own applicability definition ("a clause governing something the capability neither renders, stores, transitions, queries, nor crosses is not applicable"), these two belong in Part B as Unknown-pending-owner-N/A, not in Part A as **covered** — the Part A row labels ("no composite score is computed or served over independent answers"; "absent evidence renders Unknown, never zero and never favourable") swap the clauses' subject to make them fit (violating the file's own header rule that "the clause text is the authority"). Verification rule 5 (a citation is not a reliance) names this exactly. The requirements themselves stay fully warranted without them: SDR-36 "no cross-facet rollup" + RFC6-14's aggregate limb carry 031; VIS-2 + SDR-35 + RFC2-24 carry 034. Repair: drop RFC8-18/8-19 from the two warrant blocks, regenerate the union, and move both clauses to Part B (mapped 40→38, unmapped 284→286).

2. **CONTRACT-COVERAGE.md Part A row `RFC3-1 | an invalid governance artifact is a named failure; dependent claims render Unknown | covered | CAP1-REQ-002`: the labeled consequence is not in RFC3-1's text, and RFC3-1's actual distinctive consequence has no row.** RFC3-1 (manifests-and-namespace.md) states manifests-as-semantic-contracts and the parse-dialect rule: "two implementations disagreeing on whether one `project.yaml` parses — and therefore on whether a governance root exists — is a contradiction routed to the owner".

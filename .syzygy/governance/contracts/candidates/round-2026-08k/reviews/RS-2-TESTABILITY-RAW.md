## Blockers (max 5)

1. **CAP1-REQ-035, -036, -037, -051, -062 — declared `invariant` but missing the mandated sweep triple (CC-SPEC-4, invariant/prohibition limb).** The policy is explicit: for an invariant, the reachable case IS "the scope of quantification, a counterexample schema, and the sweep whose denominator bounds it." These five requirements' Case lines are single-fixture event cases (e.g. REQ-035: "a checker constructs a claim with two applicable conditions"; REQ-036: "a checker queries the shape answers"; REQ-051: four fixtures with no denominator) with no scope of quantification, no counterexample schema, and no sweep denominator. The spec demonstrably knows the required shape — REQ-005, -011, -013, -021, -032, -034, -041, -042, -060, -064 all carry the full triple — so these five are omissions, not a house style. Disagreement scenario: one engineer holds REQ-035 satisfied after one two-condition fixture passes; another holds it unproven over the population of multi-condition claims; the requirement text decides nothing between them. (Either the triple must be added, or the Form lines corrected to event-response/state-projection — REQ-035, -037, -051, -062 read naturally as condition→response; but as written, form and elements contradict.)

2. **CAP1-REQ-032 — the flip-matrix oracle is undecidable as stated and contradicts REQ-002, because the seven answers' constituent fact sets overlap (CC-SPEC-4: oracle must decide "without judgment").** The oracle is "flipping one answer's facts changes only that answer's value" over a 7×7 flip matrix. But REQ-030 defines Registered's constituent facts as the declaration + validation result, Shape present's as the declaration's `declarations` references, and Observable's as the coverage boundary over declared sources — the declaration is constituent to at least three answers. Any flip of Registered's facts goes through the declaration; and REQ-002 *mandates* that an invalid declaration renders "every claim dependent on the declaration `Unknown`" — i.e. flipping Registered's facts MUST change Shape present's value. A conforming implementation therefore fails REQ-032's oracle read literally; an implementation passes only under an unstated charitable reading ("flip only facts unique to one answer" — which may not exist for Registered). Concrete disagreement: engineer A corrupts the declaration, sees Shape present move to `Unknown`, and records a REQ-032 failure; engineer B calls the same observation REQ-002 conformance. The oracle needs restating (e.g. per-answer flips over disjoint evidence where they exist, plus the SDR-36 no-derivation rule checked via declared derivations as in REQ-031).

3. **CAP1-REQ-015 — the admission limb of the SHALL has no case and no oracle (CC-SPEC-4: every form names an effective oracle; the falsifier limb is unmatched by any procedure).** The requirement's third sentence — "Machine retrieval is available only to an admitted client, classified by credential presented — never by network location (RFC5-3)" — is normative, and the falsifier names "boundary facts served to an unadmitted client." But the Case produces only two successful retrievals, and the stated Oracle decides only cross-channel fact-set equality; no stated procedure produces an unadmitted retrieval attempt, and nothing at all observes "never by network location." Concrete disagreement: an implementation that admits machine clients by IP allowlist passes the stated oracle (both channels equal) while violating the SHALL sentence; engineer A passes it on the oracle, engineer B fails it on the text. Either the admission limb needs its own case + oracle (e.g. same credential from two network locations → same admission outcome; no credential → refusal), or the limb belongs to a different requirement that carries one.

## Non-blocking observations (max 5)

1. **CAP1-REQ-003 — Form line inaccurate.** Declared `state projection/query`, but the SHALL sentence is trigger→response ("WHEN a declaration omits…, Syzygy SHALL name…") and the Case is "removes a field and triggers an evaluation" — event-response. Low stakes only because those two forms carry identical element obligations under CC-SPEC-4.

2. **CAP1-REQ-036 — the expected value's spelling is undecidable by the stated "string comparison."** The oracle compares against "the deferred posture," but the requirement writes it as `not evaluated / deferred / Unknown` — never fixed as either one literal composite string or a closed set of three alternatives. Two engineers can disagree whether a served `Unknown` with a deferral reason passes. (Compounds blocker 1 for this requirement.)

3. **CAP1-REQ-023 / -053 / -061 — the prohibition sweeps' denominator is the system-under-test's own write/effect record**, in tension with CC-SPEC-4's oracle-independence element: a violating write that evades the attributed-write record (SEC-4) also evades the denominator, so the exhausted-population guarantee rests on the implementation's honesty. REQ-061 additionally quantifies over "other external effect," a population no stated means can enumerate. Stating that the checker records writes/effects externally to Syzygy (harness-level tracing or filesystem diff) would close it.

4. **CAP1-REQ-033 — falsifier wider than oracle.** The falsifier includes "facts that do not support the served value's stated basis," but the stated oracle checks only non-empty / scope-named / references-resolve — it cannot catch unsupportive facts. Either narrow the falsifier or add the support check to the oracle.

5. **CAP1-REQ-030 — the positive answer value has no fixed spelling.** `Unknown` and `Gap` are closed-vocabulary and checked verbatim throughout, but the satisfied state is described only as "satisfied when current evidence supports it"; no oracle anywhere names its verbatim rendering, leaving the third value of the domain open where the other two are closed.

On implementation independence (question d): no blocker found — the fixed entry path, the two write namespaces, and the credential rule are all owner-ruled or accepted-contract behavior (CC-SPEC-7 satisfied), and "deterministic layer" is defined by the cited accepted contracts (RFC2 core / RFC6-7: only display formatting excluded), so the REQ-005/-042 oracles are grounded outside the implementation.

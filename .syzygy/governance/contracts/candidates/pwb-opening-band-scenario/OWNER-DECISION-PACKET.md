## What is being offered

One scenario, added under PWB-REQ-010 in the signed PWB behavioral change.
It is quoted in full in `SEMANTIC-DELTA.md`; in one sentence, it says that
**if** Polaris's first reading level renders an aggregate over the Unknown
project-shape claims of one evaluation before the first capability catalog,
then there is exactly one such aggregate, it displaces no project-level
category, it carries the PWB-REQ-007 tuple and no headline status, its
population and counts equal the claims it names, every counted member stays
separately disclosed at its own claim and reachable from the aggregate, and
the machine answer carries the same aggregate.

**It is conditional, not mandatory.** Nothing in it requires the band to be
built, and a conforming rendering may still carry no opening aggregate at
all. Scheduling the work is yours (VIS-4) and P-71 already sequences slice 3
behind this scenario's sign-off, not the reverse.

**Change class: Normative**, argued in the delta rather than asserted: an
obligation is added, so a rendering that complied before may not comply now.

**It describes no write into any observed repository.** P-71-Q5 rules that
slice 6 does not run; this package neither revisits that nor relies on it.

## The three-way reconciliation, in short

P-71 conditions slice 3 on "the three-way opening-band reconciliation". The
three parties are M2's slice 2 (the currency probe), M3's slice 3 (two real
Unknowns in the first reading) and M4's slice 3 (the gap-reason band). All
three render into the region before the first catalog. The delta performs
the reconciliation by quoting each party's own design text and deriving
three agreements from it:

1. **One region, one uniqueness rule.** Three independent "renders first"
   assertions are three oracles over one piece of page order. Only a
   specification can hold this: a convention agreed between three unadopted
   design packets binds nothing.
2. **Aggregation must not cost in-place disclosure.** Every counted member
   stays disclosed at its own claim, which is the conjunction of all three
   parties' own designs and of PWB-REQ-007's "aggregates expand to members".
3. **The aggregate's population is the project-shape plane only.** M2 says
   in its own words that the probe is not a project-shape claim and that
   PWB-REQ-007 does not reach it; you ruled the same boundary generally in
   P-75 Q2. So the region may hold three blocks while the aggregate this
   scenario governs quantifies over project-shape Unknowns alone.

## Open questions — five, none of them resolved here

Where two texts pulled against each other, the reading that would have made
this change land more easily is named and **not** taken. Each of these is
yours.

### OQ-1 — one scenario cannot cover slices 4 and 5

P-71 places slices 4 and 5 "behind the same scenario". RFC2-26 requires each
observable consequence to map to an approved requirement **and** scenario,
or to carry a reviewed N/A judgment. The M4 funnel's own table records
slice 4's mapping as "None" (no requirement names the surface field) and
slice 5's as "None" (no requirement reaches the home route), both limbs
unavailable, and its three sweeps found zero hits for the surface field in
either specification.

A scenario under PWB-REQ-010 clears RFC2-26 for **slice 3 only**. P-71's
words are honoured here as *sequencing* — slices 4 and 5 wait for this
scenario — but on these bytes they cannot be read as supplying limb 1 for
them. **Not taken:** widening this scenario's WHEN until it also named the
home route and the surface descriptors, which would make one package appear
to clear all three and would pull material outside this specification's
subject inside it.

Your routes, none of which this package takes: (a) a further OpenSpec
amendment for slices 4–5, possibly inside the P-75 Q1 three-surface package
at gate bead `syzygy-dov.26`; (b) re-scoping those slices so limb 2 applies;
(c) a reviewed N/A judgment homed in `decisions/` and honored by an
effective owner act; (d) they do not run.

### OQ-2 — which requirement should host the scenario

RFC2-26's rows are "per observable consequence, not per clause". This
consequence has two halves: position and uniqueness (PWB-REQ-010's subject)
and the aggregate tuple (PWB-REQ-007's subject). Three placements:

- **(A) Under PWB-REQ-010, as drafted.** Clean of the other live changes;
  cites PWB-REQ-007 for the tuple without amending it.
- **(B) Under PWB-REQ-007.** Aligns clause and scenario for the tuple half
  but not for position, and collides on the same lines as two other live
  changes: lane B's specification patch covers the lines holding
  PWB-REQ-007's only scenario, and the P-69 Q7a clarification (gate bead
  `syzygy-dov.20`) is destined for that requirement.
- **(C) A new requirement with its own scenario.** Cleanest against
  RFC2-26, but it is not "one CC-REV-2 scenario" and it moves the
  requirement count, the proposal's totals and the coverage matrices.

**Not taken:** asserting that (A) is sufficient. It is offered as the least
colliding placement and put back to you as a question.

**Read this as a precondition, not a rounding error.** Until OQ-2 is
answered it is **not settled that this scenario clears RFC2-26 limb 1 even
for slice 3**: the consequence's position half and tuple half sit under two
different requirements, and PWB-REQ-007's only scenario is still the
per-claim case. Nothing in this package claims RFC2-26 is satisfied. If you
adopt the amendment, OQ-2 remains a condition of any later claim that slice
3's limb 1 is supplied. The 2026-09-21 review raised exactly this as its
finding 2.

### OQ-3 — the designed band does not satisfy the clause it inherits

PWB-REQ-007 requires an aggregate to disclose "separate primary/secondary
reason counts". The projection M4's slice 3 reuses counts **primary reasons
only** — the gap-reason projection in `apps/three-surface-poc/src/polaris.ts`
reads only the primary reason [Observed at source this session]. So the band
as designed would falsify this scenario's third bullet until the secondary
counts are supplied.

This delta keeps the clause's words rather than the design's. **Not taken:**
softening the bullet to "reason counts" so today's projection would pass —
that would quietly narrow PWB-REQ-007 through a scenario under a different
requirement. If you prefer the design to the clause, that is a PWB-REQ-007
amendment and belongs to the P-69 Q7a package, not to this one.

### OQ-4 — an opening aggregate against POC-REQ-032's "in place"

The three-surface specification's POC-REQ-032 requires an Unknown to be
disclosed "in place" in the narrative flow, and the M4 funnel reads that as
something "an opening aggregate is not". The scenario is drafted so the
aggregate never *replaces* an in-place disclosure. Whether that is enough —
whether a conforming rendering may carry an opening aggregate at all —
turns on a prohibition in a **different** signed specification that this
package may not amend.

**Not taken:** treating "the members are still in place" as settling
POC-REQ-032. That would be this package reading another signed change's
prohibition in the direction that lets this one land.

### OQ-5 — the order of blocks inside the region

Q7 reserves this to you expressly: "The block order *within* the band is the
owner's to set, not this packet's". Against that sits PWB-REQ-010's signed
"SHALL **first** present Butlers' purpose, promises, non-goals, architecture,
V1 scope and success criteria", and M2 slice 2's stated intent to move the
page's first human-visible instant into that region. The scenario says only
that the aggregate "displaces and defers no project-level category of this
requirement" — compatible with either order, settling neither.

**Not taken:** fixing an order in the specification, which would decide an
owner value inside a delta.

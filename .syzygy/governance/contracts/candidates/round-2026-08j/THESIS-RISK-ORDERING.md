## The question this record answers

The launch-gate's B5 asks whether the first specification is the earliest
test of the most dangerous thesis-threatening assumption — explicit risk
retirement, not merely sequencing rationale. The administration found the
sequencing rationale recorded (evidence interpretability ordering) but no
named-and-ranked assumption list. This is that list.

## What this ordering says about the launch sequence

Capability 1 is the earliest possible test of **R1 and R2** — the two
assumptions whose failure rejects the thesis outright — *provided* its
first real use is bound to a named proving project with a predeclared
evaluation plan (the administration's proposed H1; owner input P-45).
Without that binding, Capability 1 retires no risk: it would launch with
no place or protocol in which R1/R2 evidence can accrue. R3 waits on the
propagation proof by design; R4 is knowingly deferred with its waves; R5
and R6 are operational observations, not specification content.

`[Observed]` The existing sequencing rationale
(`../FIRST-OPENSPEC-SEQUENCE.md`) ordered capabilities by evidence
interpretability; this record adds the risk mapping without changing that
order — under this analysis the order survives: no later capability tests
R1/R2 earlier than Capability 1 does.

# Manifesto entry and owner walkthrough — candidate

Proposed detail for REQ-polaris-generation-023/024, alongside the existing
editorial and design acceptance requirements. This is not a walkthrough result,
owner judgment, publication act or permission to change existing bound content.

## One primary account to read and evaluate

For a governed project, the primary narrative is published at the fixed
`.syzygy/intent/OVERVIEW.md` location. Its narrative identity stays opaque; the
path is a publication location, never authority or an identity. The application
and walkthrough enter that same primary account. Additional named narratives
cannot become a competing default entry.

The entry opens with the project's supported purpose and develops a concise,
coherent argument. Contents, terminology, boundaries and supporting visuals serve
that argument under DESIGN-ACCEPTANCE.md. Review/authority disclosures must remain
clear and accessible without obscuring the reading with a persistent reference
index. Supported limits are explained meaningfully; a generic Refusals heading
is not itself evidence of comprehension.

The fixed entry keeps every applicable narrative obligation: supported and
attributable claims, explicit framing/epistemic state, profile membership,
versioned/attributed/revertible authorship, visible broken or target-changed
anchors, and machine/nonvisual parity. Every exported, embedded or plain-text
form retains presentation-artifact/non-citable metadata. A draft, publication
path or review result is never a source of authoritative project facts.

A known absent entry is a finding; an unobservable entry is Unknown with its
owning reason. Neither produces a fabricated complete manifesto. Publishing or
replacing the entry still needs the existing write, predecessor, authorship and
artifact-specific gates. A fixed destination does not authorize overwrite of
human-owned, digest-bound or otherwise protected bytes. Candidate generation can
continue separately while a conflicting publication target is unresolved.

## Discoverability is a finding, not a README write

For each declared repository of the project, assess at an identified evaluation
whether its root README links to the project entry. No configurable landing file
substitutes for that input. The read must already be admitted; this requirement
grants no new repository-body access. Missing project membership or incomplete
repository coverage stays explicit rather than yielding a complete-looking set.

The finding uses exactly yes, no, not-applicable or Unknown in human and machine
forms. An owner-declined link is no, not an error. A repository without a
governance root carries not-applicable with the declared basis. Unknown carries
its actual RFC2-24 reason and route, including unconsented-source-or-provider for
the upstream unconsented-repository branch and source-uncaptured-or-unreachable
for unavailable input. Not-applicable is this finding's value, not a navigation
outcome or an Unknown reason. A link may be proposed through its governing
Proposal mechanism; Syzygy never writes the root README link.

## Editorial readiness is not an owner verdict

Internal independent source/fidelity/design checks can make a frozen candidate
ready to offer for owner review. They cannot clear a governance review freeze or
record a human verdict on the owner's behalf. Pre-publication checks may use an
explicit candidate preview of the primary account. That preview neither replaces
the published default nor proves a walkthrough of different published bytes; every
formal run names the exact entry/version/context actually traversed. The deterministic materiality floor
covers reading order, section set, canonical diagram set, anchor targets and manifesto/thesis changes;
target-changed anchors trigger review even without prose edits.

For wording-only changes, the author may escalate to material but cannot declare
immaterial. Undeclared wording changes stay open and contestable, not implicitly
immaterial. Owner classification or contest resolution governs de-escalation.
An effective existing failure remains visible while agents prepare repairs;
drafting a repair is not permission to adopt it.

Review-failure judgments live under the governing decisions home, not in the
narrative. A clearing or holding verdict takes effect only through the applicable
owner-act predicate, carrying exact state-(1)/(2) provenance; state-(1) remains
uncorrelated. An ineffective purported pass does not clear the freeze: the failure
stays visible and the prescribed Contradiction is recorded. A stored pass or a
successful generator review cannot manufacture that authority. If no clearing
owner verdict has been submitted, the ordinary state is owner-pending with the
existing failure/freeze visible; an internal pass is not a purported owner verdict
and does not itself mint a Contradiction. A repaired candidate can be ready to
present for owner review while adoption remains frozen.

## Walk the actual page

A fresh reader with no authoring context enters the primary narrative and stays
within Polaris. Their own answers and paths must show:

- why the project exists and what it promises;
- a supported non-goal and its actual rule text;
- major capabilities and how they fit together;
- where exactness lives, reaching a verbatim requirement leaf;
- an honest Unknown and how the page communicates it;
- for a fact the reader chooses, how strongly the page claims to know it and
  what would make that claim stronger.

Then check the answers against the owning admitted sources and record divergences.
A dangling internal link on the walked path or a confident wrong answer caused
by the rendering fails. A reader's misreading of an honest page and a thin
account of an undeclared project remain judgment questions; truthful Unknowns
are not errors to conceal. The independently frozen product-completion oracle
and real-project evaluation requirements remain additional obligations.

Walkthroughs run for material narrative changes and release milestones, with at
least one nonvisual or keyboard-only path traversal per release milestone. The
test exercises movement between units, not screenshots or isolated accessible
controls alone. Performing it grants no release authority.

## Keep records and gates separate

Each walkthrough has a kernel-recorded execution record in the governing records
home, identifying surface version, evaluated context and whether it was nonvisual
or keyboard-only. The owner judgment is a separate decisions-home artifact with
verdict, rationale and judging party, honored only through the effective owner
act and rendered with its exact provenance. Neither record is stored as narrative
or replaced by the generator's Execution Record.

Missing execution evidence leaves the test Unknown-never-met. An unattributed,
unreasoned or ineffectively authorized judgment records verdict-unlawful and also
leaves it Unknown-never-met. That is an invalid judgment, not an invented failed
comprehension test. A valid judgment is never rendered Observed or as a numerical
score, and it remains a warrant rather than project evidence.

For Butlers, the adopted PWB walkthrough evaluator and its record/act-validity
population stay unchanged. Generator quality, answer completeness and readiness
are not new fields or extra arms in that evaluator. A lawful existing judgment
cannot be invalidated by inserting this generator's quality criteria into its
act predicate. Any required amendment must take its own explicit path; new
generator evidence neither replaces that evaluator nor supplies its owner act.

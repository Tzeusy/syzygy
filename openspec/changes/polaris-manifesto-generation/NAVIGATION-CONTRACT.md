# Manifesto navigation and shared answers — candidate

Proposed detail for REQ-polaris-generation-026/027. This defines the generator's
integration with the shared selection/query contract, not a second fact store,
new client permission or authority to fetch additional content.

## Explore without losing the question

From a capability, claim, diagram element or work reference, the reader can open
Why this answer?, follow its supporting records, and continue in the appropriate
surface. The selection keeps its project, durable identity, evaluation and
scenario. Returning to the narrative may restore personal reading position or
density; those presentation hints never change the resolved facts.

The host first admits the principal and authorizes project/operation access.
Only then does selection resolution apply. An unauthorized principal receives no
project counts or metadata disguised as an unconsented navigation result. Query
filters select authorized scope; they cannot grant access. Project/provider
consent remains the separate policy predicate inside authorized resolution.

## Durable selections and time

A selection names an existing kernel or project-loaded profile entity by its
owning kind and durable identity, optionally evaluation-qualified, with one
scenario context. Paths, labels, scene indices and coordinates are not identity.
Private renderer handles resolve to this reference before crossing a URL,
surface or endpoint. A presentation-profile selection is still not a valid
source-anchor target merely because it is selectable.

Unqualified evaluation means the latest identified evaluation available at
resolution, named in the answer. A pinned selection keeps its historical answer
and exposes supersession/staleness; it cannot silently move to newer source
bytes. A rename or layout change does not break the identity-based URL. Retired
identities show retirement and successor choices, never automatic redirection.
Changing or stripping a preferred-surface/view hint preserves the same selection
and answer. The same selection is openable across surfaces; lack of a surface
projection is handled explicitly rather than losing the selection.

Scenario is exactly one of the existing Base, Proposed or Historical contexts.
Base is the default; a non-default-revision Base view names that revision.
Proposed carries its base evaluation and mutually compatible selected proposal
set. Exclusive or undeclared-compatible proposals produce incompatible-scenario
and separate candidate futures, never a merged plan or aggregate. Proposed
structure stays distinct and confers no status authority. Historical names a
superseded immutable evaluation and shows staleness. Every URL, handoff and query
carries the context; a surface cannot silently substitute another.

## Total resolution, with policy first

The kernel returns the first applicable outcome in the existing precedence:
excluded, unconsented, incompatible-scenario, unresolvable, retired,
resolved-absent, unknown, resolved. No private spelling, empty panel, unexplained
error or dropped selection replaces an outcome. Not-applicable belongs only to a
surface with no projection; it does not replace the kernel outcome and is never
a kernel, URL or endpoint result. That surface still offers the shared facts and
names the surfaces where the selection projects.

Each outcome keeps its governing detail: excluded carries count; unconsented is
the standing policy state with its consent route; incompatible-scenario names the
conflict; unresolvable names the failed reference and treats a kernel-emitted
dangling link as a trust-floor incident; retired offers successors;
resolved-absent names evaluations where the identity is present; unknown carries
each governing claim's primary reason/route and separate secondary annotations;
resolved offers the complete fact set. Navigation outcomes never become claim
Unknown reasons or enter their counts.

## One Why this answer? fact set

At one selection/evaluation/scenario, all surfaces and machine answers use the
same kernel-computed fact set. Different grouping and density are allowed;
different facts, labels or provenance are not. If a consumer can answer only at
another evaluation, it names both identities and exposes the skew rather than
mixing them. Minimal display hides detail without deleting it, and machine
queries serve the complete authorized fact set. This does not mean embedding
every source body: resolvable evidence links and metadata preserve their roles,
while verbatim text remains on its separately admitted owning route.

The fact set keeps all eight RFC6-19 groups where applicable:

| Group | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Owning kind and durable identity, labels, lifecycle/succession and the governing state plane of each fact |
| Epistemic state | Governing claims' verbatim label, tier, primary/secondary reasons and freshness; suspended basis remains visible |
| Evidence | Every supporting artifact's resolvable reference, source, capture time, scope, integrity and visible revision binding |
| Provenance | Evaluation, typed answering authority, governing normative revision and observer/adapter versions |
| Warrant | Governing/motivating decisions, requirements and policies, including dismissal reason/expiry without a positive-state upgrade |
| Challenges and contradictions | Every open challenge with lifecycle and inference provenance, including submitted challenge-pending separately from admitted suspension, and pending adjudications |
| Policy | Consent, counted exclusions and the actual coverage boundary: executed mapping records plus explicitly captured scope of a partial snapshot |
| Work and reconciliation | Separate chain and normalized-work fields where work bears on the selection; uncomputed reconciliation is Unknown, never inferred from work completion |

Labels, tiers, freshness and reasons are copied from the owning evaluated state,
not synthesized by the narrative. Sibling dismissed-by-decision, unadopted-draft
and editorial-draft states remain distinct; challenge-pending travels beside the
claim tuple and does not suspend it. Internal links resolve to their identified
targets. External links remain classified external, with different availability
semantics; neither link class authorizes a body read on its own.

## Filters, aggregates and parity

Every answer names its evaluation and declared filters/scope. A partial or
filtered answer never claims whole-project coverage. Deterministic answers agree
for the same evaluation, scope and scenario. A finer textual view than a visual
aggregate is valid when its filter/aggregation difference is disclosed.

An aggregate has no label, tier, Unknown reason or freshness of its own. It gives
its membership count and expandable member composition: per-label, all six
tiers, primary Unknown reasons, freshness, sibling states, pending challenges and
applicable chain/normalized-work values. Secondary Unknown annotations are
separate and never inflate primary-reason totals. Work-state and navigation
absence values do not enter the claim-reason counts.

Equivalent renderings preserve the full evaluation/filter/graph/epistemic/sibling/
challenge/work/chain/scenario tuple. Counts over the same scope must agree.
Layout may differ, semantics may not; a disagreement is a projection/kernel
defect and cannot pass the release check. Every required selection, disclosure
and handoff remains keyboard/nonvisual operable under the presentation contract,
including the route back to the reading, not just the individual controls.

## Hard findings

- [Observed] `PWB-REQ-022` calls for a walkthrough “execution record” under `.syzygy/governance/records/`; RFC4-18 requires every Execution Record under `.syzygy/work/**`. This is a direct normative conflict, not merely missing coverage.
- [Observed] The candidate’s “authentication remains mandatory” scope statement does not cover RFC5 authentication, session, credential, exposure, audit or revocation consequences. Those are applicable and overwhelmingly uncovered.
- [Observed] `PWB-REQ-005` verifies consent and secret-policy provenance, but no requirement verifies the project-shape observer’s RFC4-7 registry entry or owner-act provenance. Body reads can therefore pass the candidate oracle while their emitting observer remains inadmissible under RFC4.
- [Observed] The candidate screens all named content sinks well, but does not specify RFC5-17’s complete exclusion record shape, redaction classes, redacted-span semantics or explicit exclusion counts.
- [Observed] Human/machine parity is strong for the candidate’s enumerated project-shape multiset. It is not full RFC6 parity: endpoint-only facts, complete epistemic tuples, aggregate composition, and mixed-evaluation behavior remain uncovered.
- [Observed] All 87 indexed RFC4–RFC6 clause identities have at least one row above; none was silently omitted.
